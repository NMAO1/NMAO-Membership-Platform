Delete Your Account

For the NMAO Member and NMAO Staff apps · NMAO Inc.

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated personal data from the NMAO Member and NMAO Staff apps, operated by NMAO Inc. We honor every valid request.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email support@nmao.us from the email address on your account, or tap the button below. Include your full name, the email address on your account, and your school / dojo name, with the subject line "Delete my account."
  2. We confirm your identity (we may ask you to verify from the email address on file) to protect your account from unauthorized deletion.
  3. We delete your personal data as described below and email you a confirmation, typically within 30 days of verifying your request.

Email a deletion request

Your account at a martial arts school is managed by that school on the NMAO platform. You may also ask the school where you are (or your child is) enrolled to submit the deletion request on your behalf — either route reaches us.

What data is deleted

When your request is verified, we delete the personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Your profile information — name, email address, and phone number;
  • Your login credentials;
  • Account records tied to your identity, such as membership, class schedule, attendance, and rank/belt-progress entries.

What may be retained, and for how long

Some information must be kept for a limited time after account deletion to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations, to resolve disputes, and to prevent fraud:

DataRetention after deletion
Payment and transaction records (required for tax & accounting)Up to 7 years, as required by law, then deleted or anonymized
Minimal records needed to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputesOnly as long as legally required, then deleted
Aggregated or anonymized data that no longer identifies youMay be retained indefinitely

Payment card numbers are never stored by NMAO — card details are handled by our payment processor (Stripe) and are not held in your NMAO account.

Deleting only some of your data

You can also ask us to delete or correct specific information without closing your account. Email support@nmao.us describing what you would like removed or corrected.
